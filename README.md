## Employer Portal Features

- **Company Profile & Account Management:** Register and manage company profiles, update industry details, logos, and contact information.
- **Job Vacancy Management:** Post new job openings, specify requirements, salaries, job types, and manage existing listings (CRUD operations).
**Applicant Tracking System (ATS):** View job applicants, inspect candidate profiles and resumes (CVs), and track application statuses (*Pending, Shortlisted, Interview Scheduled, Accepted, Rejected*).
- **Interview Scheduling & Communication:** Assign interview dates, times, and meeting details, and manage communication flow with candidates.
